Public vs Private Business
Public Business

Visible to everyone in the community and can be edited by anyone.

Pros:
  • Easy to find and discover
  • Community-driven updates for accuracy
  • Promotes transparency and sharing
  • Can be edited by customers for corrections
Cons:
  • Anyone can edit or delete information
  • Less control over content accuracy
    • Risk of vandalism or incorrect edits
Private Business

Only editable by the owner, providing more control and privacy.

Pros:
  • Full control over information
  • Protected from unauthorized edits
  • Maintains privacy and exclusivity
  • Only you can update or delete
Cons:
  • Less discoverable by the public
  • Limited community contributions
  • May not appear in public searches

Recommendation: Choose Public if you want maximum visibility and community involvement. Choose Private if you need full control and privacy for your business information.
