What is WIKONOMI?

Empowering Papua New Guineans with transparent pricing information

Our Mission

Transparent Pricing

Living in PNG is tough, with the cost of living and prices of goods and services constantly rising. We believe you deserve to find the best products at the best prices with just a click.

Community-Driven

Built by Papua New Guineans, for Papua New Guineans. This platform helps you discover the best products and services at competitive prices, making informed purchasing decisions easier.
